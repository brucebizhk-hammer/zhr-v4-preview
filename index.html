04 — HOW WE WORK

Every transaction stands
on three pillars.

Standard documentary settlement, independent inspection, and a fully documented transaction flow — on every cargo, without exception.

DLC

Irrevocable DLC (MT700)

Payment by letter of credit, payable against documents. Issued through top-tier international banks. No advance payments, no exceptions.

SPA

Standard Procedure

Standard SPA terms, fully documented transaction flow. Clear on product spec, quantity, price, delivery, payment and arbitration. No hidden clauses.

SGS

Independent Inspection

SGS or CCIC inspection at loading. Both sides receive signed certificates before shipment. Disputes resolved by analytical test results.

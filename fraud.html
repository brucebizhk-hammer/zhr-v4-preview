FRAUD AWARENESS

Commodity trading attracts fraud. We talk about it in the open.

Serious trading firms are transparent about fraud because they have nothing to hide — scammers never build a page like this. Read it before your next deal, whether you trade with us or not.

Common scam patterns

If you spot any of these on any deal, stop.
01
Fake sellers

Non-existent "mandates" or "allocations" with forged inspection reports and non-responsive suppliers.

02
Advance-fee traps

Any request for "registration", "allocation", "legalisation" or "bank guarantee" fees up front is a red flag.

03
Identity theft

Scammers copy real company details, websites and certificates to impersonate legitimate firms.

04
Document forgery

Fake SGS reports, certificates of origin, and bills of lading that circulate in the trade.

What we will and will not do

Read this before you deal with us — or with anyone else in this market.
We will
  • Work under a mutual NDA framework
  • Communicate only from zhrtrading.com
  • Accept irrevocable DLC (MT700) at sight
  • Submit to SGS or CCIC inspection at load port
  • Provide verifiable corporate references
  • Meet you on video at any point, no NDA needed
We will not
  • Ask for any upfront fees of any kind
  • Accept "soft probe clauses" or unverified procedures
  • Sign contracts with non-verifiable counterparties
  • Offer prices significantly below market
  • Use free email domains or anonymous proxies
  • Promise delivery dates we cannot evidence

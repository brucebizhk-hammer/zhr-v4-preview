COMPANY · WHO WE ARE

A commodity desk built from the plant floor up.

ZHR Trading is a cross-border commodity trading platform connecting international producers, mines and processors with China's chemical industry — and Chinese producers with buyers across Asia-Pacific, the Middle East, Africa and Europe.

Both directions, one book.

Raw materials in, finished chemistry out — the same desk sees both sides of the price.

We buy raw materials — sulphur, phosphate rock, potash, fluorspar and other bulk minerals — from producers and authorised traders worldwide, and we sell finished chemical products: phosphate fertilizers, phosphoric acid, phosphate fine chemicals and new-energy materials, across China and the Asia-Pacific.

ZHR was built by one of China's earliest professional commodity electronic spot trading teams, with more than a decade of hands-on experience across the full chemical chain — plants, mines, processing facilities, warehouses and sea shipping — combined with institutional trading discipline.

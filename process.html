PROCESS & TRADE STANDARDS

Our process is the product.

What we sell is not a cargo — it is a repeatable, fully documented way of moving cargo. The terms below apply to every transaction, for every buyer and every seller, without exception.

How we work

Three pillars stand behind every contract we sign.
DLC

Irrevocable DLC (MT700)

Payment by letter of credit, payable against documents, issued through top-tier international banks. No advance payments, no exceptions.

SPA

Standard procedure

Standard SPA terms and a fully documented transaction flow. Product specification, quantity, price, delivery, payment and arbitration all in writing. No hidden clauses, no side letters.

SGS

Independent inspection

SGS or CCIC at loading port. Both sides receive the signed certificates before shipment. Any quality dispute is settled by analytical test results.

Transaction flow

From first enquiry to settlement — the same five steps on every cargo.
01
Enquiry & NDAProduct, quantity and destination. Mutual NDA before commercial detail is exchanged.
02
Terms & SPASpecification, price basis, delivery and payment set in a single written agreement.
03
InstrumentBuyer issues the DLC (MT700). We confirm and register it with our producer.
04
Load & inspectSGS or CCIC at load port. Certificates issued to both sides before sailing.
05
SettlementDocuments presented against the instrument. Funds released on compliant docs.

Six trading principles

These are not marketing lines. Each one is a limit we accept on our own behaviour.
Documentation before volume — nothing moves until the terms are on paper and signed.
Instrument-backed settlement only. We do not trade on trust and we do not ask anyone to.
Specification is fixed at contract, not at load port.
Third-party inspection at both ends, paid by whoever the market convention points to.
One named counterparty per transaction — you always know who is responsible.
Refuse the trade when the paperwork does not add up, whatever the margin.

Risk & price management

Price risk is part of the commodity business. We manage it openly rather than pricing it into guesswork.
Pricing

Benchmark-anchored

Contracts tied to recognised public indices rather than negotiated in the dark.

Hedge

Tenor-aligned

Hedging duration matched to each cargo's shipping schedule — never a speculative book.

Monitoring

Basis & spread tracking

Continuous monitoring of regional price spreads, landed cost and freight.

Discipline

Exposure limits

Open positions governed by internal risk policy. No trader discretion on size.
